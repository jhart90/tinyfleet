A cozy little world with no edge of the map

TinyFleet is a driving game set in a tilt-shift miniature world that keeps going for as long as you do. Islands and continents are made as you reach them, each with its own towns, coast and weather. Pick a road and follow it.

Free, in your browser. Nothing to download or install.

What is out there

  • Towns in more than twenty styles. Paris boulevards, Tokyo neon, Amsterdam canals, Chicago towers, San Francisco’s painted ladies, the Vegas Strip, Tudor villages, medieval walled towns, onion-domed Russian cities, Ottoman bazaars, Maya pyramids, Japanese hot-spring towns and more, each with its landmarks.
  • Wonders and oddities. Castles, lighthouses, ancient wonders, a temple on a karst island, covered bridges, and thirty strange things in the middle of nowhere.
  • Big country. Mesas, canyons, great waterfalls, river deltas, dunes and ice. Ski resorts with working lifts, beach resorts, theme parks with coasters that run, stadiums and race circuits.
  • Seasons and weather. Autumn colour, blossom, snow, evening fog, rain and thunderstorms, and a day and night that change how every town looks.

How you travel

You start in a little red car. Deliveries along the way pay for the next vehicle: a convertible for the coast road, a yacht or a paddle steamer for the rivers, a biplane, a helicopter, a hot-air balloon, a submarine.

You choose which jobs to take and when, and nothing stops you from ignoring the work and driving. Hire a driver or two and they will follow you, so you are never the only car on an empty road.

Keeping what you find

  • The Passport. A booklet of the sights you have seen and the towns you have visited, with stamps to collect.
  • Photo mode. Frame a still or a short clip and send it as a postcard link anyone can open in their browser.
  • Marks. Sign a guestbook or put up a sign, and find the ones other players left.
  • The map. A region map that fills in as you survey each county.

A world with other people in it

It is one shared online world. You will see other players’ cars pass and find their names on the bridges they built, but nobody can get in your way, and you can play the whole thing on your own.

Questions and answers

Is TinyFleet a relaxing game?

It can be. Deliveries have clocks, but you choose which to take, and you are free to simply drive and explore.

How big is the world?

It has no edge. New islands and continents are generated as you travel towards them.

Is every town different?

Towns come in more than twenty architectural styles, with different layouts, landmarks and surroundings, so no two are the same.

Can I take screenshots and share them?

Yes. Photo mode takes stills and short clips, and a postcard link opens the game at the place you photographed.

Do I have to play with other people?

No. Other players share the world, but you can explore entirely on your own.
