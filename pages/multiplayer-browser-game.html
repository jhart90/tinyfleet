A multiplayer browser game where everyone shares one world

TinyFleet is not split into matches or lobbies. There is one persistent online world, and every player’s company is in it: you pass other fleets on the road, drive over bridges other people built, and find your own name in the standings.

One world, everyone in it

Sign in and you are in the same world as everybody else. Other players’ cars, trucks, boats and aircraft are out on the same roads, rivers and runways as yours. Click one to see whose it is and what it is carrying, or follow it with the camera.

The world carries on between your visits. Towns grow with the trade players bring them, and what was built yesterday is still there today.

Co-op: share a fleet with friends

A shared fleet is one company run by several people: one purse, one headquarters, one name and colours. Start one and send your friends an invite link or a code. Each of you drives and manages when you can, and the company belongs to all of you.

Shared fleets have officers, and people can ask to join. You do not have to be online at the same time as your friends, because the fleet lives on the server.

A world players change

  • Roads, bridges and railways. What a player builds is there for everyone to use, with the builder’s name on it.
  • Signs, guestbooks and cairns. Leave a mark at a place you found, and read the ones others left.
  • Hearts. Give one to a road or a building you like.
  • Postcards. Frame a view in photo mode and send it as a link that opens the game at that spot, in the browser, for anyone.

Competing without fighting

There is no combat in TinyFleet. You compete through trade and driving: who carries the most for a town, who is worth the most, who holds the lap record.

  • Standings and leaderboards. Net worth, deliveries and more, you against every other fleet.
  • Lap records and ghosts. Set a time at a race circuit and others race your ghost.
  • Challenge lines. Timed sprints by road, air and sea, measured by the server, with a board for each.
  • Weekly titles. Named honours for the fleets that did the most that week.
  • Stadium leagues. One league table for the whole world, with teams and touring acts your fleet can carry.

Friends and neighbours

Right-click any name to add a friend. The Social window keeps your friends, your fleet, the companies working near you, an inbox and the leaderboards in one place, and the local newspaper reports what the firms in your county have been up to.

Questions and answers

Is TinyFleet co-op?

Yes. Several players can share one fleet, with one purse, one headquarters and one set of colours, and run the company together.

How do I play with a friend?

Start or open a shared fleet, then send your friend its invite link or code. You can also just add each other as friends and run separate companies in the same world.

Do we have to be online at the same time?

No. A shared fleet is kept on the server, so each of you can play whenever you like.

Can I play alone?

Yes. You can run your own company and never deal with another player. Computer-run rival firms work the same towns either way.

Is there player-versus-player combat?

No. Competition is through trade, standings, race times and challenge boards.

Does it cost anything?

No. TinyFleet is free and runs in the browser.
