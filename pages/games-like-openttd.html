A game like OpenTTD, played from the driver’s seat

If you like OpenTTD and Transport Tycoon for the feeling of a transport company growing across a map, TinyFleet is after the same feeling from a different seat. You build the company, lay the roads and the track, and then drive the vehicles yourself, in 3D, in a browser, in a world you share with other players.

What it has in common

  • Road, rail, sea and air. Trucks and buses, trains, ships and aircraft in one company.
  • Cargo between towns and industries. Each town makes some things and wants others, and pays for what you bring.
  • Towns that grow. A village you keep supplied becomes a town with a dealer and a station.
  • Your own infrastructure. Roads, bridges, tunnels, railway track, junctions and stations.
  • Rival companies. Computer-run firms compete for every town.
  • Multiplayer. Other people’s companies on the same map.

TinyFleet is the lighter game. It does not have OpenTTD’s depth of network design, its signalling puzzles or its mods. What it adds is being down on the road: you take the first truck out yourself, see the towns at street level, and watch the fleet you hired follow you over a bridge you built.

Who will like it

Players who enjoy the early game of a transport tycoon most: the first few vehicles, the first profitable line, a town starting to grow. Players who want a transport game to dip into in a browser. And players who want to share a company with friends without setting up a server.

Questions and answers

Is TinyFleet a clone of OpenTTD?

No. It is its own game with a similar subject. You run a transport company by road, rail, sea and air, but you drive the vehicles yourself in a 3D world shared with other players.

Can I play it in a browser?

Yes. TinyFleet runs in a browser tab with nothing to download or install.

Does it have multiplayer like OpenTTD?

It has multiplayer of a different kind: one persistent online world that every player is in, with shared fleets for playing co-op.

Can I build railways and stations?

Yes. You can lay track with junctions and raised sections, and build six kinds of station.

Does it have signals or mods?

No. A dispatcher handles signalling, and there are no mods.

Is it free?

Yes.
