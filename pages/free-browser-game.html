A free browser game with a whole world in it

TinyFleet is a free 3D driving and delivery game that runs in a browser tab. There is nothing to download, nothing to install and no launcher: press Play and you are in a little red car with a parcel to deliver.

Press Play and you are driving

Open the game, choose a name for your fleet and a PIN, and you start at the edge of a town with your first delivery waiting. The first few jobs teach the controls as you go: drive with the keyboard, pick up at one ring, drop off at another, get paid.

From there it opens out. The money buys a second vehicle and a driver to go with it, then a headquarters, then trucks, buses, trains, boats and aircraft. The world keeps going in every direction, so there is always another town down the road.

What is in the free game

  • A world with no edge. Islands and continents generated county by county, with towns in more than twenty architectural styles.
  • More than 70 vehicles. Cars, vans, trucks and buses, trains, ferries and yachts, helicopters, biplanes and jets.
  • A company to build. Hired drivers, a headquarters, property, and rival firms after the same trade.
  • Things to build. Your own roads, bridges, tunnels and railway lines.
  • Other people. One shared online world, with friends, shared fleets and leaderboards.

What it runs on

A desktop or laptop computer with a keyboard, and a current browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The game draws in 3D with WebGL, so the browser’s graphics acceleration needs to be on, which it is by default. An Auto quality setting adjusts the detail to keep the game smooth on modest laptops.

TinyFleet is not made for phones. If you would rather have it in its own window than in a tab, there are free apps for Windows and Mac on the home page.

Your progress is kept

Your company is saved on the TinyFleet server under your fleet name. Sign in with the same name and PIN on any computer and it is all there: your vehicles, your drivers, your money and the roads you built.

Questions and answers

Is TinyFleet really free?

Yes. The browser game is free to play, and the Windows and Mac apps are free downloads.

Do I have to download anything?

No. The game loads and runs in a browser tab. The Windows and Mac apps are optional.

Do I need an email address to sign up?

No. You choose a fleet name and a PIN, and that is the whole sign-up.

Can I play on a phone or a tablet?

TinyFleet is made for a desktop or laptop with a keyboard, and is not made for phones.

Is it a single-player or a multiplayer game?

Both, in a way. Everyone plays in the same online world, but you can run your own company without ever dealing with another player, or share a fleet with friends.
