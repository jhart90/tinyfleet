A game like Euro Truck Simulator that runs in your browser

The part of Euro Truck Simulator that keeps people playing is the loop: take a load, drive it across the map, get paid, buy a better truck, hire a driver, grow the company. TinyFleet is built on that loop, as a small, bright, free browser game with other players on the road.

What it has in common

  • Hauling for a living. Pick up freight in one town and deliver it to another, against the clock.
  • A company that grows. One vehicle becomes a fleet, with a headquarters that grows with it.
  • Hired drivers. They take vehicles out on their own and bring money in while you drive.
  • A garage of trucks. Vans, tippers, mixers, tankers and semis, with parts to fit in the workshop.
  • Long roads. Highways, canyon roads, sea bridges and city streets, by day and by night.

TinyFleet is not a simulator. There are no gears, mirrors or weigh stations, and nobody will mistake it for a real road. It is the trucking-company loop in miniature, quick to start and easy to leave running in a tab.

More than trucks

The same company can run buses, trains on track you lay yourself, ferries, helicopters and jets. Your fleet can drive in convoy behind you and lend you its holds for one big run. Rival firms compete for every town, and you can buy property, win territory and buy the rivals out.

Who will like it

Players who like the business side of a trucking game as much as the driving. Players on a laptop that will not run a full simulator. And players who want something to play with friends in a browser, with nothing to buy or install.

TinyFleet is its own game and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SCS Software or the Euro Truck Simulator series. The names are used only to compare.

Questions and answers

Is TinyFleet a truck simulator?

No. It is a light driving and logistics game about running a haulage company, with simple driving and a miniature look.

Is there a free game like Euro Truck Simulator?

TinyFleet is free and shares its loop of hauling loads, earning money, buying vehicles and hiring drivers, though it is a much lighter game.

Can I play it without downloading?

Yes. It runs in a browser tab.

Can I hire drivers?

Yes. Hired drivers work on their own to the orders you give, or follow you in convoy.

Does it have multiplayer?

Yes. Everyone plays in one shared online world, and friends can share a fleet.

Will it run on a laptop?

It is made for desktops and laptops with a keyboard and a current browser, and an Auto quality setting adjusts detail to keep it smooth.
