TinyFleet in one paragraph

TinyFleet is a free online driving and logistics game played in a web browser. You start with one small car and a parcel to deliver, and build a transport company from there: hiring drivers, buying trucks, trains, boats and aircraft, laying your own roads and railway lines, and competing with rival firms for the trade of every town. It all happens in one shared, procedurally generated world with no edge, drawn as a low-poly, tilt-shift miniature, where every player’s fleet is on the same roads.

In one line: a transport tycoon you drive yourself, free in your browser, in a world you share.

What is in it

  • 73 vehicles across road, water, air and rail, all drivable. The full list.
  • 286 kinds of cargo, from parcels and passengers to freight, bulk and containers. The full list.
  • 60 town styles, from Parisian boulevards to Tokyo neon. The full list.
  • 10 factions to work for or fall out with. The full list.
  • Building: roads, bridges, tunnels, railway track and stations.
  • A company: hired drivers, a headquarters, property, territory and rival firms.
  • A shared world: other players’ fleets on the same roads, shared fleets for co-op, leaderboards, race records and postcards.
