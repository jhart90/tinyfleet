Factions

10 factions run through every town in TinyFleet. Each has work it pays for and things it will not forgive, and a fleet’s standing with each one opens paintjobs, vehicles and favours.

The factions

FactionWho they areThey pay for
🚓 The ConstabularyEvery town’s police: the patrols on their beats, the stations, the courts and the cells.Evidence and prisoners carried, cadets to the stations, stuck patrol cars towed, smugglers brought in.
🎩 The FamilyNobody says the word. The social clubs, the chop shops, the villa on the hill and every load that moves after dark.Night runs made, protection rounds, laundered cash, stolen goods to the chop shop.
🏛️ The MinistryThe state above the towns: halls, ministries, customs, embassies, the Mint, and a hangar nobody talks about.Diplomats, delegates, bonded loads, cash for the banks, state visits, red tape, ballot boxes, and wreckage brought to Hangar 18.
🛸 The VisitorsLights over the mesas, rings of stones in the high country, a crash nobody reported. They want their things back.Lost probes and wreckage brought to a landing ring, samples to the observatories, a delegation to a town hall at 3 a.m.
👷 The BrotherhoodEvery building site, every road crew and every cement mixer in the world answers to the hall.Cement, panels, roadstone, pipes and rebar to the sites, crews to the work, and every road, bridge and earthwork you pay for.
📯 The PostThe sorting offices, the rounds, the island boats and the night mail plane.Mail bags, parcels, island post, air mail, the morning post before nine, registered mail without a knock.
🎖️ The ArmyBases, forts, depots and airbases, convoys on the roads and fighters in the sky.Soldiers, rations, field kit, ordnance and recruits; parades; wreckage to Hangar 18.
🎬 The StudiosFilm lots, backlots, premieres and the stars who live in the hills.Stars, crews, props and dailies; red carpets and premieres.
🎣 The GuildThe fishing harbours, the markets, the net lofts and the lifeboats.Fish, catch, ice and bait; boaters rescued; the island post by boat.
📰 The PressThe news stations, the printworks, the radio masts, the live trucks and the eye in the sky.Scoops, crews and footage from every live event; the morning edition before seven.

Standing

Standing with a faction climbs through 7 rungs: Outcast, Hostile, Wary, Neutral, Friend, Honoured, Sworn. The better the standing, the better its work pays.
