TinyFleet › Guide

Controls

TinyFleet is played with a keyboard and mouse. In the game, every control is on the sheet behind the keyboard button in the corner of the screen.

Driving

W A S D or the arrow keysDrive and steer
ShiftNitrous, once fitted
Hold SpaceHandbrake
KHorn, once bought at a Vehicle Shop
NHeadlights off and on (a helicopter’s searchlight)

Cargo, the world and other vehicles

Tap Space or clickLoad cargo, or enter a shop or tavern
TabBeside one of your own vehicles: take its wheel
GIn a Dealer’s or Vehicle Shop’s striped ring: open its counter
Click a vehicle or buildingShow its card

Camera

Q E or right-dragOrbit
PPitch
Scroll wheel or + −Zoom in and out
RLock the camera behind the car
CPhotos and clips

Menus and tools

HHeadquarters
FFleet
JJournal
IStandings
OSettings
MIsland overview
YRegion map
BBuild roads
XBulldoze
TTerrain

Trains

W and S move the throttle, which stays where it is set: S goes through stop into reverse. A and D take the left or right branch at a turnout.

Try them

The first few deliveries teach the controls as you go.
