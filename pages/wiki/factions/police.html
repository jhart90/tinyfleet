🚓 The Constabulary

Every town’s police: the patrols on their beats, the stations, the courts and the cells.

Working for them

Also known asThe Police
Gives the workDetective Sergeant
They pay forEvidence and prisoners carried, cadets to the stations, stuck patrol cars towed, smugglers brought in.
They hateNight runs, contraband, protection money.

Your standing with them

Every fleet has a standing with each faction, from Outcast at the bottom to Sworn at the top: Outcast, Hostile, Wary, Neutral, Friend, Honoured, Sworn. Work they pay for raises it, and what they hate lowers it.

At Hostile and belowA patrol that sees you by day pulls you over and fines you; checkpoints stop you clean or not.
At HonouredPatrols wave you through their checkpoints.
When SwornK is a siren in any car you drive: the traffic ahead pulls over for you.

Friends and enemies

Standing gained with one faction spills over to the others: a little is gained with its allies, and some is lost with its rivals and more with those opposed to it.
