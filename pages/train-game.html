Your railway

  • Lay your own track. Draw lines the way you draw roads: junctions, parallel track, raised sections on viaducts, and six kinds of station, from a country halt to a city terminus.
  • Seven locomotives. Steam tank engines, freight diesels, a silver streamliner, a high-speed express, a long-haul heavy and a mountain cog railway.
  • Drive them yourself. Bring the train into the platform, load, and take it down the line.
  • Hire drivers for every line. Give a train its stops and it works the line while you drive something else.
  • Station-to-station freight. Loads tell you how many stops ahead they are going, and the route follows the rails.

A working network

The world already has railways when you arrive: main lines between the cities, branch lines up the valleys, level crossings where the gates come down for you. Your trains share that track with everyone else’s, and the dispatcher holds trains for each other at junctions and single-track sections, so there is no signalling to wire up.

Some towns run their own commuter railways in their own colours. In winter, look out for the Christmas train, and for a few others that are on no timetable.

The country it runs through

Mountain passes, river gorges, long sea crossings, autumn forests and snowfields, and towns in more than twenty styles from Amsterdam canals to Tokyo neon. Every crossing is a real bridge or viaduct, and five famous bridge designs turn up on the great ones.

More than trains

The same company runs road vehicles, boats and aircraft. A railway does best with trucks feeding its stations, and you can drive any of them. If the management side is what you are after, see TinyFleet as a transport tycoon.

Questions and answers

Can I build my own railway?

Yes. You can lay track, junctions, parallel lines and raised sections, and build six kinds of station.

Can I drive the trains?

Yes. You drive any train in your fleet yourself, or hire a driver to work the line for you.

Do I have to place signals?

No. A dispatcher holds trains for each other at junctions and on single track.

What kinds of train are there?

Seven locomotives: steam tank engines, freight diesels, a streamliner, a high-speed express, a long-haul heavy and a mountain cog railway.

Is it multiplayer?

Yes. It is one shared online world, and other players’ trains run on the same network.

Is it free?

Yes, in your browser, with nothing to download.
