TinyFleet 0.7.7: Guests Welcome

Version 0.7.7 · 6 November 2026

A smaller update: jump straight in without signing up, a fix for built railways that could vanish, and hired jets that go looking for work. As always, the world stays exactly as it is.

🚗 Play as guest

  • The sign-in screen has a new Play as guest button: no name, no PIN, you're driving in seconds.
  • While you play as a guest, a red tile at the top of your missions reminds you. Click it, pick a name and a PIN, and your fleet is saved for good, with everything you've done so far.

🚆 Built railways fixed

  • Undoing a piece of track could make your whole built network disappear the next time you loaded the game. That's fixed, and networks hit by it come back on your next load, joined the way you laid them.
  • Any track you had undone may come back with them: bulldoze it (X) if you don't want it.
  • Earthworks carried over a county line no longer move the ground under a railway, building or harbour sitting on the line.

✈️ Smaller changes

  • Hired jets that find no work at their airport for two minutes now fly empty to the nearest airport that has some, instead of waiting there forever.
  • Toppers on planes, helicopters, blimps and balloons sit properly on top, and are big enough to see.

Thanks for playing, and for the reports that led to these fixes (the pause menu's Feedback button comes straight to me). See you on the road! 🚗
