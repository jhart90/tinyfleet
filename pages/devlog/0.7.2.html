TinyFleet 0.7.2: Your Own Railway, and Gold for the Passport

Version 0.7.2 · 29 October 2026

0.7.2 is another big one, and again the world stays exactly as it is: your fleet, your HQ and everything you've built are right where you left them. You can now lay your own railway, haul the biggest loads in the game, take bands and teams on tour, and finally get something special for filling your Passport.

🛤️ Lay your own railway

  • The builder (B) has a new Rails side. Lay track the way you lay roads, join it to the lines already there, and put up stations: halts, town and city stations, termini, goods yards and harbour depots.
  • Hold Shift to lay a second track alongside a line, so trains can pass each other. The bulldozer takes track and stations up again.
  • A new lesson, A Line of Your Own, shows you how. It turns up after three train deliveries, or the first time your train meets another one head on.

📦 Bigger loads, bigger money

  • Ultra-Bulk work: loads of 50 to 100 units for the four biggest holds in the game, Big Blue, the Workhorse, the Longhaul and the Jumbo, between harbours, stations and airports they can really reach. Look for the hazard lime and black.
  • Long-haul work for jets and trains: loads worth $5,000 to $19,999, bound five or more counties away, shown in gold with a pillar of light.
  • Freight, and any load of 8 units or more, pays half as much again.
  • Hired drivers with big holds chain several loads into one run, and prefer loads that are going their way.

🎸 On tour

  • Touring bands and league teams need taking on to their next stadium, the real next date on their tour or fixture list. Carry them by bus, by your own train or by Jumbo, and get them there before the show or the kick-off.

🪩 Gold for the Passport

Fill a page of your Passport to the last stamp and it's sealed in gold, with a treat of its own:

  • Wonders: the Wonder Ball. A mirror ball on a chrome mast on the roof of any vehicle you choose. It turns as you drive, catches the sun by day, and throws spots of coloured light all round you after dark. Visitors to a wonder also pay you 10% more.
  • Town styles: Architect's Eye. Dress your HQ in any town style you've stamped.
  • Biomes: the Expedition livery, and your own vehicles lose less speed off the road.
  • Town sizes: Skyline decals, and passengers pay 5% more.
  • Landforms: the Explorer's lantern, amber headlamps and a roof searchlight, and 10% more for new ground.
  • Buildings: the Blueprint collection. Put up any kind of building you've stamped as scenery, from the builder's Scenery side.
  • Events: the Front-row pass, a lanyard on your fleet badge, a confetti horn, and event prizes 10% higher.
  • Signature buildings: a gilded nameplate on your HQ and a fanfare horn.
  • Each page of visas: that group's emblem as a frame round your fleet badge.

And for the few who fill every stamp in the book: the Gilded Fleet. Every vehicle and building you own in gold (trim or the full works, your choice), a gold name, a Gilded title on the Standings, and a gold statue of your favourite vehicle outside your HQ. Everyone online hears about it.

The workshop (G) puts the treats on each vehicle, and the HQ window has a new Treats tab for the rest. Other players see what you're wearing.

🏆 24 new achievements

  • Haulage by weight and by hold (Heavy Hauler, Freight Forwarder, Bulk Carrier, Box Clever), the long and the ultra hauls, your best single delivery, and bands and teams carried.
  • Railway building: track laid, stations built, junctions, double track, how high and how far your train goes, and the Mountain Goat's climb.
  • Submarine loads and dives, jumps landed, boost pads, lap records held, and bridges across the strait to the next county.

🌉 Long bridges

  • Bridging from one county to the next over the water is much easier now, for every kind of road, four-lane and interstate included.

👀 Watching other players

  • Following a player puts the maps away, shows you the same drop rings they can see, and draws their train properly, with a steady camera.
  • A loading card with a progress bar while a far-away county is built, and far follows load in about half the time.
  • Following someone in a jet now stays on the jet, however far it flies.

🏁 Fair laps

  • Lap records can't be set by cutting across the infield any more: more than a wheel past the kerb and the lap doesn't count. Records already set that way come off the boards the next time someone races that circuit.

🔧 Fixes and polish

  • Chat reopens whispering to whoever you last whispered to, and chat lines stay up longer.
  • The top-left corner opens the menu and the Players list, and the Standings tabs wrap instead of running off the edge.
  • CPU and hired drivers get the same surge from boost pads that you do.
  • A race circuit HQ shows its picture in the HQ window again.
  • Big careers save safely: saves are compressed, and the server keeps much larger ones.
  • Hired jets show what they're carrying in the dock bubble, and you can see their drops when you follow them.

Thanks for playing, and for all the feedback (the pause menu's Feedback button comes straight to me). See you on the road! 🚗
