TinyFleet 0.7.1: Every Vehicle Has a Job

Version 0.7.1 · 27 October 2026

0.7.1 is a big update on top of last week's new world, and the world stays exactly as it is: your fleet, your HQ and everything you've built are right where you left them. Most of what's new is about giving you more to do with the vehicles you already have, and more to aim for in the jobs you already know.

🚜 Every vehicle has a job

Fourteen working vehicles now get missions of their own, offered only while you're driving them:

  • The Dozer: the mayor wants a boarded-up eyesore gone. Ram it hard enough and it comes down in a cloud of dust.
  • The Medic: a patient needs the hospital, and the clock starts the moment they're aboard. Sound the siren (K) to clear the road and earn a bonus.
  • Hooky: breakdown recovery. A car has given up on the road out of town; tow it in to the garage.
  • The post vans: a round of postboxes before the last post.
  • Sundae: park at the busy spots round town and sell ice cream.
  • The Sunrise balloon: scenic flights at dawn or dusk, over the sights and down in a field.
  • The Puddle Duck: duck tours, from the town square straight into the harbour and back.
  • The Marshmallow blimp: live TV coverage, held steady over the stadium during the big match.
  • The Gator airboat: swamp tours through the reeds and mangroves.
  • The Serenade yacht: wedding charters along the coast.
  • The Delta Belle: river cruises, calling at landings upstream.
  • The Defender: night patrols round the harbour, hunting for night runners.
  • The Barnstormer: crop dusting, low over the farms.
  • The Mountain Goat: walkers up the rack railway to the village on the mountain.

🌊 Under the Waves

  • Submarines can work the seabed. Dive over a seabed site, settle on the bottom and load up: wreck finds, strongboxes, lost containers, specimens and more, each with its own look.
  • Buoys mark the sites, and the water clears round a dived sub so you can see what you're doing.
  • New submarine missions, and the Still Waters story now takes you under the sea.

🥇 Medal times

  • Every delivery mission now has bronze, silver and gold times, set the moment your first load is aboard, from the route and the vehicle you're in.
  • Beat them for a bonus on top of the usual pay: +15%, +35%, or +60% and a diamond for gold. Finish slower and you're paid just what you always were.
  • The mission chip counts down to the next medal, the journal keeps your best on every job, and there are two new achievements to chase.

🚚 Convoys

Three one-off missions for growing fleets, each needing your own vehicles in line behind you (F, then Follow):

  • Moving Day at 2 vehicles: a family's whole house, too much for one hold.
  • The Circus Comes to Town at 4: move the big top and lead the parade.
  • Relief Convoy at 6: supplies for a village cut off, against the clock.

💼 Business

  • Buy out the competition. Any building a CPU firm owns can be bought from them at four times its price, or you can buy the whole firm (its buildings, drivers and vehicles) at three times what it's worth.
  • Grade your own towns. In a town that's your territory, the terrain tool can now reshape its open ground.
  • The tech tree is redrawn as clear rows (Sightseeing, Fares, Racing, Haulage, Deep, Rotors…), with no crossing lines, and arrow keys move along a row.

☄️ Visitors from above

  • After a meteor shower, the stones lie where they fell for a day. Bring them back to your HQ as the Visitors' cultural goods and your town takes on a little of their culture.

🎓 Learning as you grow

New teaching missions turn up when you're ready for them, with tooltips pointing the way:

  • building your first road (at a level 3 HQ), and your first bridge over the water;
  • levelling rough ground with the terrain tool (at a level 4 HQ);
  • unlocking a vehicle on the tech tree (once you've earned 24 diamonds);
  • asking a town you hold for a new building.

🏁 PvP and the map

  • The "Sixty" lines are now Distance lines, and the sign at each start says which fleet holds it.
  • On the region map (Y), other players' fleets show in their own colours.
  • Your hired jet pilots appear on the region map, and you can click and watch them from the dock.

🔧 Fixes and polish

  • Route arrows to a place in another county now lead to a real way across (a road, a bridge, a causeway or a car ferry) instead of off the coast into deep water.
  • Mission rings and cargo rings show through buildings that fade out in front of the camera.
  • PvP start lines and mission rings lie properly on slopes and roads, with no flicker.
  • A clear "press Enter to start" prompt whenever a mission is ready to begin.
  • Following another player's vehicle no longer shows an empty screen.
  • Seven new glass towers, after famous ones, so city skylines are less samey.

Thanks for playing, and for all the feedback (the pause menu's Feedback button comes straight to me). See you on the road! 🚗
