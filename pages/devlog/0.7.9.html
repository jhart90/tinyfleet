TinyFleet 0.7.9: Made by You

Version 0.7.9 · 8 November 2026

Your buildings go to work. Own a mill and it mills; own the bakery down the road and your flour comes back as bread. A new first drive teaches the keys while the world loads, and a batch of freezes are gone. As always, the world stays exactly as it is.

🏭 Supply chains

  • Owned buildings make goods. Every mill, dairy, sawmill, paper mill, bakery, steelworks and nearly a hundred kinds in all now has a recipe: deliver its inputs to the door and a run starts on the clock; a base plant (a dairy, a quarry, a cabin) simply makes on its own. The made load waits at the kerb, in your fleet's colour, for your drivers first; leave it a while and the island's haulers take it and you still get the maker's fee.
  • Chains. Twenty-five of them, two or three steps each: timber to lumber to furniture, pulpwood to paper to books, milk and ice to ice cream, crude to fuel, scrap to steel to new cars. A good made from your own goods is worth more at every step (up to +60% with three steps of pedigree behind it), and the dearest made goods pass $3,000 a load.
  • Routes. A vehicle card's Set a route lets you right-click a line or loop of your doors; the driver runs it, carrying what each stop has for the next.
  • Fetch. Each bin on a plant's card has a Fetch button: the nearest free driver that can carry it goes looking for that kind of load and brings it in, working normally in between.
  • Vehicles with a trade. The Mule is a horsebox (+20% on horses and livestock), the Bramble carries plantation goods, the Zippy is a despatch rider (+30%), Big Bertha a flatbed for timber, steel and panels, the Stomper hauls whisky, the Sundae keeps ice cream cold and pays +30% on it, and the Clod pays on farm goods. No chain involves the jets.
  • Loads with a job. Building sites take ready-mix, panels and asphalt and pay a tenth of the building when yours tops it out. Fireworks pay more to a show that's on. A launch pad fed telemetry sends a rocket up. The Dozer's knock-downs leave rubble for the crusher. Glass, fireworks, a racehorse and instruments are fragile: a hard knock takes a tenth off.
  • Plants show a bar in the world and on their deed card; the HQ Estate pane has a chains strip, a Plants filter and a Next load sort; a town's Buildings tab gets an Industry section. Five new deeds: Production Line, Made by You, Cone Zone, Lap of Honour and Vertical. The paper writes up a plant's 50th, 250th and 1,000th load.
  • A few chains have nowhere to stand yet (a bindery, a creamery, a winery…): those buildings arrive with the new world in 0.8.0.

🛣️ The first road

  • A new player's loading screen is now a lit road in the dark. While the world is made you learn W, take a bend with A and D, stop for a sign with S, then try the camera keys, so you arrive knowing how to drive.
  • The welcome card docks at the side with the keys in view and the car free to drive. It no longer closes when you press W.
  • Helping Hand comes a little later, the handbrake lesson waits for a level 2 HQ, and Put her to Work is shown by a click guide.

⚡ Fewer freezes

  • Opening the garage, the Tech window or the HQ's Fleet tab no longer stalls for seconds while the vehicle pictures are drawn: they fill in as you watch, and are kept for next time.
  • A click in the road or rail builder no longer plans the whole county in one go: it searches a little each frame toward where you point.
  • More shaders and the biggest buildings are warmed up under the loading screen, and the game makes about a quarter less garbage as you play, so there is less for the browser to sweep up mid-drive.

🔧 Smaller changes

  • A train can change ends while it is stopped.
  • The HQ's Diamonds columns show three rows each and scroll when opened, leaving the tree more room; a vehicle's Mode comes before its cut.
  • The Tribune never carries two near-identical stories, and its bylines get two lines.
  • Cut hillsides are lit by the day: they no longer glow at night or show a field of pale dots. The tow card no longer flashes white in dark mode.

Thanks for playing, and for the reports that led to these fixes (the pause menu's Feedback button comes straight to me). See you on the road! 🚗
